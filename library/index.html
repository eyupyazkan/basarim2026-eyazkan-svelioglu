MXene library

Hypothetical Mn+1XnTx structures and their GCMC results. Choose a family, then the M, X and Tx sites; the structure opens in 3D.

Data: M.Sc. thesis of E. Yazkan (Gebze Technical University, 2026; advisor S. Velioğlu), appendix tables. Structures optimized in Materials Studio, atomic charges DFT-ESP (DMol3). Element data: pymatgen; atom colours Jmol/CPK. Viewing only; the library will be released.
